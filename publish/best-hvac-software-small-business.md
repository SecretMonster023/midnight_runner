## Verdict

**Jobber** is worth considering if you operate solo or have a small crew and want straightforward quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payments. **Housecall Pro** deserves a closer look if HVAC-specific price-book tools and routing matter more. **ServiceTitan** may suit businesses that need more configurable operations and reporting, but its quote-based sales process makes costs harder to compare upfront.

These recommendations are based on vendor documentation, not hands-on product testing. Confirm current plan inclusions before committing.

## HVAC software at a glance

| Software | Best starting point | Main tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Jobber | Solo and small teams | Add-on costs |
| Housecall Pro | HVAC-focused crews | Tier-dependent features |
| ServiceTitan | Complex operations | Quote-based pricing |

The right choice depends less on a headline feature count than on the number of people who need access, your dispatch workflow, and the full monthly cost after add-ons.

## Jobber: simpler day-to-day workflows

Jobber covers core operations such as quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payments. Its [pricing and plan comparison](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) distinguishes options for owner-operators and teams. Review the [Core plan documentation](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/) and [Connect plan documentation](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/) to check which workflows and user allowances fit your business.

**Where it may fall short:** Optional services and additional users can change the total cost. Ask about every add-on you would need, rather than comparing the base plan alone. A growing multi-location operation should also confirm whether its desired workflows are supported.

## Housecall Pro: HVAC-oriented tools

Housecall Pro promotes HVAC-oriented workflows, including a [Price Book feature](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro). That may be useful if your technicians repeatedly quote common jobs and need more consistent pricing in the field.

Check [Housecall Pro's current plans](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/) for included users and feature availability. The company's [field-service overview](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/) also describes automation and other tools, but advertised capabilities should not be assumed to be included in every tier.

**Where it may fall short:** Some advanced tools may require higher-tier plans. Ask specifically about API access, AI features, and the exact price-book capabilities available to your team.

## ServiceTitan: more configurable operations

ServiceTitan positions itself around more extensive operational tools, with package options described on its [official pricing page](https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing). Businesses prioritizing advanced dispatching, reporting, or broader administrative processes may want to evaluate it.

**Where it may fall short:** Public pricing is not enough to calculate an exact total for your team. Request a written proposal that specifies licensing, onboarding, modules, contract terms, and support before comparing it with other platforms.

## How to choose for your crew

For a solo operator, start by checking whether Jobber's entry-level workflow covers the entire process from quote to payment. For a small HVAC team, compare that workflow with Housecall Pro's price-book and dispatch features. If your operations are already complex enough to require customized reporting and administrative controls, add ServiceTitan to the shortlist.

Run the same sample job through each product you trial: create an estimate, schedule a technician, record work completed, send an invoice, and record payment. A consistent test reveals workflow friction that feature lists do not.

## What to verify before you buy

- **Total cost:** Get written pricing for your exact number of office users and technicians, plus add-ons and onboarding.
- **Dispatch and routing:** Confirm the tools your actual team needs are included in the quoted tier.
- **Price book:** Test a real HVAC job with your labor, materials, and pricing structure.
- **Accounting and payments:** Verify integration behavior, payment fees, and how transactions appear in your accounting software.
- **Contract and support:** Ask about commitments, migration help, training, and cancellation terms.

Start with two demos or trials that match your crew size. Only add a third platform if the first two cannot meet your must-have workflows.
