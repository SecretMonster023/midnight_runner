## Verdict
- Jobber: best for solo plumbers and very small crews who need low entry cost and fast setup—start with Jobber’s Core or Connect plans and verify included users and add‑ons ([Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/); [Core plan](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/); [Connect plan](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/)).  
- Housecall Pro: best if you want trade‑specific Price Book defaults, built‑in marketing/review tools and bundled AI intake—evaluate Essentials or MAX and test Price Book/AI in a demo or trial ([Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/); [field service overview](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/); [Price Book docs](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro)).  
- ServiceTitan: best for growing multi‑tech shops that need per‑technician licensing, advanced analytics and enterprise modules—request a demo and written quote before committing ([ServiceTitan pricing](https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing)).

## Who each product fits
### Jobber
- Size: solopreneurs to very small crews. Core plan is single‑user; higher plans or per‑user add‑ons apply ([Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/); [Core plan](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/)).  
- Strengths: straightforward scheduling, quoting, invoicing, integrated payments and QuickBooks Online sync (feature availability depends on plan).  
- Poor fit: enterprise governance, multi‑location franchises or shops that need many advanced modules without contacting sales.

### Housecall Pro
- Size: solo to small/medium teams (pricing page shows Basic, Essentials, MAX examples with differing included user counts) ([Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)).  
- Strengths: plumbing Price Book defaults, review and marketing tools, AI intake/CSR AI and consumer financing options.  
- Poor fit: teams that require open API access or specific benchmarking features on low‑cost plans—some advanced features are limited to higher tiers.

### ServiceTitan
- Size: 10+ techs or rapidly scaling shops that need enterprise features.  
- Strengths: per‑technician licensing model, deep pricebook/dispatch/reporting modules and many Pro add‑ons.  
- Poor fit: single‑tech operators or very small shops that need immediate, transparent per‑month pricing online.

## Meaningful drawbacks and poor‑fit cases
- Jobber
  - Core plan is single‑user; teams need higher tiers or paid user add‑ons—confirm per‑user costs.  
  - Some advanced features are add‑ons or reserved for higher tiers; not ideal for heavy cross‑location governance without sales involvement.

- Housecall Pro
  - Marketing claims about revenue uplift and hours saved are vendor statements—treat them as claims and ask for references ([Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/); [field service overview](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/)).  
  - Certain benchmarking, Pricing Insights and API access are limited to higher plans—confirm plan inclusions for your use case ([Price Book docs](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro)).

- ServiceTitan
  - Public site requires contacting sales for exact per‑technician pricing and contract terms—no simple public per‑seat price to compare quickly ([ServiceTitan pricing](https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing)).  
  - Modular, feature‑rich setup can increase total cost and onboarding time if you adopt many add‑ons.

## What to verify before you buy
- Final monthly/annual price, billing cadence and any promotions; get a written quote.  
- Exact user/seat definitions and per‑user charges for mobile/office accounts ([Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)).  
- Which features are add‑ons or limited to higher tiers (marketing suites, Pricing Insights, receptionist/AI, open API) and their costs.  
- Payment processing rates, payout timing and instant‑payout fees for your merchant/country setup ([Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)).  
- Integration specifics and accounting sync behavior (QuickBooks/Xero field mapping and plan availability) — request documentation ([Jobber Connect plan](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/), [Housecall Pro overview](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/)).  
- Trial/demo length, onboarding fees, contract terms, cancellation policy and support SLAs—get these in writing. ServiceTitan requires a demo/contact for pricing and contract details ([ServiceTitan pricing](https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing)).
