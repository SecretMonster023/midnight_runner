# Jobber vs Housecall Pro: Which Is Better for a Small Contractor?

Affiliate disclosure: Trade Business Lab may earn an affiliate fee if you click a vendor link and buy a paid plan. We don’t let that influence our coverage.

Bottom line
- Jobber is a cleaner, trade-focused pick if you want a straightforward, lower-priced entry for a solo operator, simple quote-to-invoice workflows, and easy QuickBooks/Xero sync with clear per-user add-ons. See Jobber pricing and details. (Jobber pricing)
- Housecall Pro is stronger if you need built-in dispatch, route optimization, live fleet GPS, a prebuilt trade price book, and an AI toolset (including optional CSR AI) aimed at scaling from small teams to larger operations. See Housecall Pro pricing and features. (Housecall Pro pricing)

Who each product fits — quick guide
- Jobber is a good fit when:
  - You’re a solo pro or a very small contractor and want a single-user entry plan (Jobber Core supports 1 user). [Jobber Core plan](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/) [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)
  - You prioritize straightforward quoting, client hub, invoicing, and integrated payments (Jobber Payments is available in the US, UK, and Canada). [Jobber Core plan](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/) [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)
  - You want a clear upgrade path to crew scheduling, job costing, and custom reporting on higher tiers. [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)

- Housecall Pro is a good fit when:
  - You need built-in dispatch, route optimization, live GPS tracking, and field-focused tools in the same product family. [Housecall Pro features](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/) [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)
  - You value a prebuilt, editable price book and industry defaults for faster estimating. [Housecall Pro Price Book docs](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro)
  - You plan to use AI intake/assistant tools (Housecall Pro advertises multiple built-in AI tools and a CSR AI add-on). [Housecall Pro field service overview](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/) [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)

Meaningful drawbacks and poor-fit cases
- When Jobber may be a poor fit
  - If you need built-in, full dispatch with route optimization and live fleet GPS as core features — Housecall Pro emphasizes dispatch/GPS more directly. [Housecall Pro features](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/) [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)
  - If you expect many paid seats and want lower per-user rates — Jobber charges add-user fees ($29/mo each) that can raise subscription cost as you scale. [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)

- When Housecall Pro may be a poor fit
  - If your highest priority is the lowest entry monthly price for a single-user plan — Jobber’s Core lists a lower billed‑annually starting price. [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)
  - If you need an unconstrained count of included users at a low cost, review Housecall Pro’s included user counts and additional-user pricing for your expected team size. [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)

Practical decision framework — choose in 5 minutes
1. Team size: Count users who need app/scheduling access. If you’re solo, compare Jobber Core ($29/mo billed annually) vs Housecall Pro Basic ($59/mo billed annually). If you need 5+ users, add seat costs and compare Jobber Connect/Grow vs Housecall Pro Essentials/MAX. [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)
2. Dispatch & fleet: If live GPS, route optimization, and dispatching are core to your daily operations, prioritize Housecall Pro. [Housecall Pro dispatch/GPS](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/)
3. Estimating & price books: If a prebuilt trade price book and editable industry templates save you setup time, Housecall Pro’s Default Price Book is helpful. If you prefer Jobber’s quote-to-job workflow and job-costing on higher tiers, check Jobber Grow/Plus features. [Housecall Pro Price Book docs](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro) [Jobber pricing/features](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)
4. Payments: Compare card, ACH/bank, and instant-payout fees based on your expected transaction mix. [Jobber payments](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) [Housecall Pro payments](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)
5. Test both: Use each vendor’s 14-day trial to import a couple of real jobs, run a quote, schedule a crew, and test mobile workflows. [Jobber trial](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) [Housecall Pro trial](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)

Summary
Both Jobber and Housecall Pro serve home‑service contractors. Jobber leans toward a simpler path for solo pros and very small teams with clear per-user pricing and strong quote/invoice workflows. Housecall Pro emphasizes dispatch, fleet/GPS, a built-in price book, and an AI toolset for firms that need field operations and intake automation at scale. Use the five-step decision framework above and verify seat counts, integrations, and payment fees for your business before signing up.
