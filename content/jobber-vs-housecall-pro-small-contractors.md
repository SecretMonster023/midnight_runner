Practical decision framework (how to choose in 5 minutes)
1. Team size and seats: Count users who need app/scheduling access. If you’re solo, compare Jobber Core ($29/yr-billed) vs Housecall Pro Basic ($59/yr-billed). If you need 5+ users, compare Jobber Connect/Grow vs Housecall Pro Essentials/MAX and add up seat costs. [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/).
2. Dispatch & fleet: If you need live GPS, route optimization, and dispatching as core tools, prioritize Housecall Pro where those features are central. [Housecall Pro dispatch/GPS](https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/).
3. Estimating & price book: If you want a prebuilt trade price book and editable industry templates, Housecall Pro’s Default Price Book is a time-saver. If you prefer Jobber’s quote-to-job workflow and job-costing on higher tiers, check Jobber’s Grow/Plus features. 4. Payment flows & costs: Compare card, ACH and instant payout fees for each vendor against your expected transaction mix. Jobber lists per-method fees and instant-payout terms; Housecall Pro lists processing starting rates and Instapay. [Jobber payments](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), [Housecall Pro payments](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/).
5. Try both: Use each vendor’s 14‑day trial to import a couple of real jobs, run a quote, schedule a crew, and test mobile workflows (both vendors offer 14‑day trials per their pricing pages). [Jobber trial](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), [Housecall Pro trial](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/).

Final note
Both products are built for home‑service contractors and have overlapping feature sets. Jobber leans toward a simpler scale path with clear per-user pricing and a strong quote/invoice/workflow for small teams; Housecall Pro emphasizes dispatch, fleet/GPS, price books, and an AI toolset that targets growth and intake automation. Use the five-step decision framework above and validate the specific seat, API, and payment-fee details for your business before signing up.
