How each product fits — by company size, trade, complexity, and buying priorities
- Small solo operators or 1-person side-gigs
  - Jobber Core is explicitly positioned for solopreneurs and supports 1 user on the Core plan [Core plan doc] (https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/). That can be attractive when you want a low entry price and essentials bundled.
  - Housecall Pro Basic also targets solo operators with a single included user; vendor materials present Basic as “for getting started” [pricing] (https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/).
- Growing teams (2–10 techs)
  - Jobber’s Connect and Grow tiers add multiple included users and features (Connect supports up to 5 users on its standard description; higher tiers include more) and Jobber lists per-user add-on pricing ($29/mo per extra user) [pricing & Connect doc] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/.
  - Housecall Pro Essentials and MAX include more users (Essentials includes 5 users; MAX includes 8) and list per-additional-user fees on MAX ($35/mo) [pricing] (https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/).
- Trades that benefit from pre-built pricebooks and mechanical defaults (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical)
  - Housecall Pro documents Default Price Books tailored to trades and notes mechanical industries get a more extensive default price book; Pricing Insights (market benchmarking) is available on the Max plan for mechanical packages [Price Book doc] (https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro).
  - Jobber supports quoting and optional line items and image-rich quotes, with more advanced quoting on Grow/Plus; it’s a flexible quoting tool but the vendor documentation does not advertise a pre-built trade-specific pricebook the way Housecall Pro does [pricing/features] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/).
- Businesses prioritizing AI intake and built-in coaching/analytics
  - Housecall Pro documents five AI tools (CSR AI, Analyst AI, Marketing AI, Coach AI, Help AI) and describes CSR AI (AI customer service rep) as an add-on for intake/booking; the vendor frames these as platform differentiators [field-service overview] (https://www.housecallpro.com/field-service-management-software/).
  - Jobber offers Jobber AI Voice and Jobber AI Chat and a Receptionist add-on; Jobber emphasizes AI voice/chat for field workers and an AI receptionist as an add-on on certain plans [pricing; Core plan doc] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/.

Meaningful drawbacks and poor-fit cases (from vendor docs + explicit analysis)
- When Housecall Pro may be a poor fit
  - If you need to minimize per-user ongoing cost and plan to scale beyond included users without paying the vendor’s listed per-user fees, verify the incremental user pricing and total cost (Housecall Pro lists $35/mo per extra user on MAX) [pricing] (https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/). This may make larger head counts more expensive versus other vendors depending on your plan choices.
  - If you need free form or highly-customized price lists outside Housecall Pro’s Default Price Books and prefer not to enter prices manually: Housecall Pro’s Price Book is pre-populated with service descriptions but vendors state “you will need to enter your own pricing” for those services [Price Book doc] (https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro).
- When Jobber may be a poor fit
  - If you’re buying because you want a built-in trade default pricebook and mechanical benchmarking, Jobber’s documentation doesn’t advertise a Default Price Book or market-based Pricing Insights equivalent in the way Housecall Pro does; Housecall Pro explicitly documents those trade-specific pricebook features [Jobber pricing/features vs. Housecall Pro Price Book] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro.
  - If you want every AI intake capability bundled without add-ons, Jobber positions Receptionist as an add-on and shows AI tools available across plans but at extra cost or limited to higher tiers; verify which AI features require add-ons or higher plans before buying [pricing & Core plan doc] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/.

- Payment processing fees, ACH/instant payout fees, and whether instant payouts are optional with an extra percentage fee (both vendors document payment features but fees can be region- and account-dependent).

Practical decision framework — how to pick between them for your small contracting business
1. List the non-negotiables for your operation (e.g., trade pricebook/flat-rate quoting, number of users in first 12 months, QuickBooks sync, instant payouts, AI call-answering).
2. Match those non-negotiables to vendor docs:
   - If pre-built trade Price Books and Pricing Insights for mechanical trades matter, prioritize Housecall Pro’s Price Book features as documented [Price Book doc] (https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9778163-how-to-use-price-book-by-housecall-pro).
   - If a low-cost single-user starter plan and per-user add-ons that are cheaper per seat are priorities, Jobber’s Core/Connect structure may be a better fit [Jobber pricing & Core doc] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/.
3. Calculate 12-month TCO (subscription + expected add-on fees + per-user fees + payment processing fees) for both vendors using your realistic headcount and then compare — vendor pages list the plan prices and many add-on costs to support this calculation [Jobber pricing; Housecall Pro pricing] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/.
4. Use the free trial from each vendor (both advertise 14-day trials) to verify workflow fit and to confirm which features are included during trial and which require add-ons [pricing & field-service overview] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/.
5. Before committing, confirm onboarding support and whether white-glove onboarding or a dedicated specialist is included or paid (Jobber advertises dedicated onboarding/premium support options on higher tiers; Housecall Pro advertises dedicated onboarding on MAX) [Jobber pricing; Housecall Pro pricing] (https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/.
